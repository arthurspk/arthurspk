## 🎯 What is Guia Dev Brasil

**Guia Dev Brasil** is a network of open source study guides, written in Portuguese, that gathers learning paths, courses, books, channels, tools and communities for anyone who wants to get into tech or grow in it. Everything is free, community-maintained and open to contributions.

- **154 public guides** on this profile, covering fields (front-end, back-end, cybersecurity, data, mobile, networking…) and technologies (JavaScript, Python, Docker, Linux, AWS…).
- **Over 27 thousand stars** combined and **over 9.9 thousand followers** on GitHub (September 2026).
- **GDB Quality Standard v2:** every link is verified, content is always legal, Portuguese and free resources come first, and every guide gets an English translation.
- Daily content on Instagram [@guiadevbrasil](https://www.instagram.com/guiadevbrasil/) and videos on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCzmXzz_VR0Li8-YOvWN_t3g).

All guides are listed at [github.com/arthurspk?tab=repositories&q=guia&sort=stargazers](https://github.com/arthurspk?tab=repositories&q=guia&sort=stargazers), sorted by stars.

## 📌 Where to start

The six repositories pinned on the profile are the best starting point — begin with the main guide and move on to your field:

1. [guiadevbrasil](https://github.com/arthurspk/guiadevbrasil) — the main guide: a map of every tech field and the entry point to the network.
2. [guiadofrontend](https://github.com/arthurspk/guiadofrontend) — complete front-end path (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, frameworks).
3. [guiadecybersecurity](https://github.com/arthurspk/guiadecybersecurity) — information security: courses, certifications, CTFs and roadmaps.
4. [guiadobackend](https://github.com/arthurspk/guiadobackend) — complete back-end path (APIs, databases, languages).
5. [guiadeferramentas](https://github.com/arthurspk/guiadeferramentas) — tools and extensions for a developer's daily work.
6. [guiadetypescript](https://github.com/arthurspk/guiadetypescript) — the first guide rebuilt under Standard v2: a sample of the format every guide will follow.

## 🚨 How to contribute

Guia Dev Brasil only exists because of its community. Four ways to help, from the quickest to the most involved:

1. **Star ⭐** the guides you use — it helps other people find them.
2. **Report a broken link** or outdated resource by opening an issue on the guide in question (every guide has a *Broken link* template).
3. **Suggest a resource** (course, book, channel, tool, community) on the guide for that topic, with a working link, legal content and one line saying why it is worth the click.
4. **Translate or review** a guide's English version (`translations/README.en.md`).

Suggestions and fixes for **this profile** (showcase, statistics, translation) follow this repository's [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). By participating, you agree to our [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
